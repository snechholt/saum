# Candidate manager protocol

The reusable procedures are [PROMPT_EVALUATE.md](PROMPT_EVALUATE.md) and [PROMPT_SYNTHESIZE.md](PROMPT_SYNTHESIZE.md). They define review operations and output structure. [CAMPAIGN.md](CAMPAIGN.md) identifies the project-specific sources, scope, acceptance requirements and decision ownership. Replace the campaign when applying the procedures elsewhere.

Evaluation derives an acceptance basis and a salience register from authoritative inputs. It assesses experiments independently, then uses comparative evidence to complete causal review of consequential decisions. Most avoidable or unsupported proposals remain in REVIEW_TRACE.md with their disposition. HUMAN_FINDINGS.md contains only vetted residual concerns and explicitly required disclosures; high salience alone does not imply human escalation.

Review the evaluation before synthesis in a fresh context. Synthesis preserves the attention filter and prepares qualified learnings and informative guidance. Neither procedure repairs experiments or adopts authority changes.

For a discovery test, supply the campaign, its authoritative sources and its experiment evidence. Keep prior evaluations, benchmark answers, expected conclusions and regression reports outside the evaluator's inputs. Preserve the resulting review trace before comparing with the benchmark. A regression whose reviewer has already seen the expected finding is a source-grounded check, not evidence of blind discovery.

Project-specific examples and regression results belong outside these reusable procedures. Procedural categories, necessity tests and approval obligations come from current authority rather than accumulating in the prompts.
