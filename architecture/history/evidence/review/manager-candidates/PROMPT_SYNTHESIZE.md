# Manager synthesis pass

Read CAMPAIGN.md, the reviewed EVALUATION.md and HUMAN_FINDINGS.md in a fresh context. Consult REVIEW_TRACE.md and immutable source evidence when needed. Recover the acceptance basis, decision ownership and high-salience categories from their cited authority; do not import categories or approval rules from prior campaigns. Do not repair experiments or change authority in this pass.

## Produce LEARNINGS.md

Separate established findings, qualified local mechanisms, demonstrated failure modes, unresolved interpretations, and proposed next actions. Preserve evidence, confidence, successful scope and tradeoffs. Useful local evidence can survive rejection of an experiment's completion claim; it does not establish the validity of the whole result.

Do not turn every implementation mistake into a permanent rule, combine sibling mechanisms into a prescribed architecture, or treat an expensive approach as inefficient without an equivalent valid comparison. Distinguish authority-derived obligations from options and hypotheses.

Use the evaluation's vetted human findings. Do not resurrect suppressed proposals, convert a source-derived exceptional category into a universal manager rule, or promote a hypothesis through summarization. If synthesis discovers a materially new concern, apply the same authority derivation, causal review and attention filter before including it. Record its review trace rather than forwarding it raw.

For a surviving proposal, identify the affected owner, what remains unsettled and the evidence required for the next transition. Adoption follows the supplied authority and outer workflow; local usefulness alone does not settle a mechanism.

## Produce PROPOSED_EXPERIMENT_GUIDANCE.md

Prepare compact, informative context for a future experiment, subordinate to its authoritative inputs and scope. Include established pitfalls, qualified options and selected evidence references. Do not make it a task list or a source of new obligations.

Keep mandatory corrections, changes to authority or scope, unapproved obligations and unresolved consequential decisions in their designated review channels. Do not smuggle them into optional guidance or inject the guidance into an experiment automatically. Follow any explicit routing requirements in the supplied authority.

## Preserve the attention filter and uncertainty

Keep the human-facing account focused on concerns that survived causal review, with the concrete decision or useful action visible. Retain suppressed items in the audit record; do not expand them back into the notification payload. Respect source-required disclosures without presenting them as endorsement.

Carry forward missing evidence, untested behavior, partial mechanisms, absent valid comparisons and unproved cost attribution. A proposed validation should state its question, held-constant obligations and discriminating evidence without preselecting the answer. Neither synthesis nor optional guidance changes authority.
